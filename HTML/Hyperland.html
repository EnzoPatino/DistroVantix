Hyperland

Hyperland is a science fiction novel written by Douglas Adams. It was published posthumously in 2020, and it is a collection of essays and stories that explore the concept of hyperreality and the nature of reality itself. The book delves into topics such as virtual reality, artificial intelligence, and the impact of technology on society. It is a thought-provoking read that challenges readers to question their perceptions of reality and the role of technology in shaping our world.
